# Dragonfly Scanner — User Guide

**For customers who want to identify Dragonfly products and earn loyalty points.**

Live at: <https://dragonfly.cannacrypted.com>

Add it to your phone's home screen (Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen; Chrome: ⋮ → Install app) for the fastest experience.

---

## What can I do?

1. **Scan a product.** Point your camera at any Dragonfly preroll, flower, vape, or disposable and get strain details — genetics, lineage, flavor, effects, terpenes.
2. **Earn loyalty points.** Snap a photo of your dispensary receipt. For every dollar you spent on Dragonfly products, you get 1 point.
3. **Check your balance and history** any time.

You do **not** need to download an app or create a password. Your email *is* your loyalty account.

---

## Scan a product

1. On the home screen, tap **Scan Product**.
2. Point the camera at the label and hold steady — the green frame is your aim zone.
3. If your camera doesn't open (permission denied, in-app browser, etc.), tap **Upload a photo** and pick one from your gallery.
4. Results appear in a few seconds: strain name, THC range, full genetics and lineage, flavor profile, and effects.

**Tips:**
- Good light, single product in frame, no glare on the label.
- If the product isn't in our database, you'll still get what we could read from the label.
- Scanned something that isn't Dragonfly? You'll get a snarky take on it and a suggestion for a comparable Dragonfly strain, plus nearby dispensaries.

---

## Get your points

1. Tap the **Scan Your Points** button (the big one at the top).
2. Enter your email and confirm you're 21+. First time, your account is created automatically. Returning? You stay signed in on this device.
3. Tap **Scan My Receipt** to open the camera — or **Upload Photo Instead** to pick from your gallery.
4. Capture the whole receipt — retailer name at the top, every line item, and the total at the bottom all visible.
5. Points appear immediately. You'll see your new balance, which items counted as Dragonfly, and which didn't.

### How points are awarded

- **1 point per $1** of Dragonfly product subtotal on the receipt.
- Tax, delivery fees, and non-Dragonfly items don't earn points.
- Points round down. $18.99 of Dragonfly = 18 points.
- You get your points instantly — no waiting for approval.

### Optional: share your location

On the scan screen, there's an "I'm at the dispensary (include location)" checkbox. Turning it on captures your GPS coordinates with the receipt. It's optional and has no effect on points earned — it just gives the Dragonfly team signal on where scans are happening.

### Rules and limits

- **Each receipt can only be submitted once.** If you try to scan the same one twice (or a friend tries to submit it), the second attempt is blocked.
- **Receipts over $500 total, rapid-fire scanning (3+ in 5 min), or anything unusual** gets flagged for review. Points are still credited immediately — flagged scans may be reviewed by the Dragonfly team later.
- **Photos that can't be read** don't earn points. Try a clearer shot.

- Current point balance
- Every receipt you've submitted, with date, retailer, total, and points earned
- Any voided or under-review receipts marked clearly

Tap **Sign out** to clear your session from this device.

## Troubleshooting

**Camera doesn't open.**
Your browser may have denied camera permission. Look for a camera icon in the address bar and allow it. Or tap **Upload Photo Instead** — same result, using a photo from your gallery.

**"That doesn't look like a receipt."**
The image was unreadable — too dark, blurry, or not a full receipt. Try again with better light, the receipt flat and fully in frame.

**"This receipt has already been submitted."**
Someone (possibly you, on a different device) already scanned this exact receipt. Each physical receipt can only earn points once. If you think this is wrong, DM the Dragonfly team.

**"Session expired. Sign in with your email to continue."**
Sessions last 30 days. Tap through the sign-in screen again — your account and points are still there, you just need a fresh session.

**I scanned but my points are wrong.**
- Check the line items on your result screen — the scanner marks which items it thought were Dragonfly. If a Dragonfly item was missed, message the team with the receipt and account email.
- If an adjustment is approved, the Dragonfly team can add points to your account manually.

**I lost my phone / I want my points on a new device.**
Sign in with your email on the new device. Points are tied to the email, not the device.

## Privacy

- We store your email, points balance, and the receipts you submit.
- Receipts include the retailer, date, total, line items, and (if you opted in) location coordinates.
- We don't share this data with third parties.
- The image you scan is sent to our product-recognition service (Anthropic Claude) for parsing and isn't stored by us long-term.
- Delete your account or data by emailing the Dragonfly team.
